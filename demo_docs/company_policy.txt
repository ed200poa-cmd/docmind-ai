SECTION 1: WORK HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Standard working hours at ACME Corporation are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time. Employees are expected to be present and ready to work at the start of their shift. Flexible work arrangements may be approved by a direct manager on a case-by-case basis.

Employees must notify their manager at least one hour before their shift begins if they will be late or absent. Repeated unexcused absences (more than 3 in a rolling 90-day period) may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

Remote work is permitted for eligible positions. Remote-eligible roles are determined by department heads in consultation with HR. All remote employees must maintain core availability hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone.

SECTION 2: VACATION AND TIME OFF
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Full-time employees accrue paid vacation at the following rates:
- Years 0–2: 10 days per year (0.83 days/month)
- Years 3–5: 15 days per year (1.25 days/month)
- Years 6+:  20 days per year (1.67 days/month)

Vacation requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. Requests during peak business periods (December 15 – January 5 and July 4 week) require four weeks advance notice and manager approval.

Unused vacation days may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar year. Any unused days above the 5-day limit are forfeited on January 1.

SECTION 3: HEALTH AND WELLNESS BENEFITS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ACME Corporation provides comprehensive health benefits to all full-time employees working 30 or more hours per week. Benefits become effective on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment.

Medical Insurance: ACME offers three plan tiers — Bronze, Silver, and Gold — through BlueCross BlueShield. ACME pays 80% of employee premiums and 50% of dependent premiums. Employees may add spouses, domestic partners, and dependent children up to age 26.

Dental and Vision: Basic dental and vision coverage is included at no additional cost. Enhanced plans are available at employee expense.

Mental Health: All employees have access to 12 free counseling sessions per year through our Employee Assistance Program (EAP). Sessions are confidential and can be scheduled through the EAP hotline at 1-800-555-0199.

Wellness Stipend: Each employee receives a $500 annual wellness stipend, reimbursable for gym memberships, fitness equipment, meditation apps, or nutrition counseling. Submit receipts through the expense portal.

SECTION 4: CODE OF CONDUCT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All ACME employees are expected to conduct themselves with integrity, respect, and professionalism. This includes interactions with colleagues, clients, vendors, and the public.

Harassment Policy: ACME has zero tolerance for harassment of any kind, including but not limited to harassment based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, or sexual orientation. Any employee who experiences or witnesses harassment should report it to HR immediately via the confidential reporting portal at hr.acme.internal or by calling the HR hotline.

Conflict of Interest: Employees must disclose any outside business interests or relationships that could conflict with their duties at ACME. Approval from the Ethics Committee is required before accepting gifts valued over $50 from vendors or clients.

SECTION 5: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS AND COMPENSATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Performance reviews are conducted twice yearly: mid-year in June and annually in December. Employees receive written feedback from their direct manager, and have the opportunity to provide self-assessments.

Merit increases are tied to performance ratings. Employees rated "Meets Expectations" or above are eligible for an annual merit increase of 2–4%. Employees rated "Exceeds Expectations" may receive increases of 4–8% plus a discretionary bonus.

New employees are not eligible for merit increases in their first year of employment but will receive a performance review at the 6-month mark.

SECTION 6: PARENTAL LEAVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ACME offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all full-time employees who have been with the company for at least 12 months. This applies to all parents — birth, adoption, and foster — regardless of gender.

Parental leave must begin within 12 months of the child's birth, adoption, or placement. Employees are encouraged to give at least 30 days notice before leave begins. Upon return, employees are guaranteed the same or equivalent position.

SECTION 7: EQUIPMENT AND TECHNOLOGY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ACME provides all full-time employees with a laptop computer, keyboard, mouse, and monitor. Employees may request additional equipment (standing desks, ergonomic chairs, secondary monitors) through the IT portal, subject to manager approval and budget availability.

Company equipment is for business use only. Personal use of company devices should be minimal and must not compromise company security. Employees must not install unauthorized software. All devices must have disk encryption enabled and automatic screen lock set to 5 minutes or less.

Upon separation from the company, all equipment must be returned within 5 business days.
